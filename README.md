# 🌱 Project Green

### Smart Land Information & Verification System

**Bridging the gap between geographical locations and accessible land information.**

Project Green is an innovative location-based platform that aims to simplify land research by connecting real-world geographical locations with relevant land-related records.

Users can capture a photograph of a land parcel, automatically obtain its geographical coordinates, and use integrated APIs and available government data sources to retrieve relevant property information.

### 🎯 Our Vision
To make land information more accessible, transparent, and easier to understand, helping individuals make informed property-related decisions.

### ✨ Key Objectives
- 📍 **Geolocation-Based Identification:** Identify land locations using GPS coordinates.
- 📸 **Geotagged Photography:** Capture and associate photographs with geographical locations.
- 🔗 **Smart Data Integration:** Connect with available land-record databases and government APIs.
- 🏡 **Property Information Access:** Retrieve relevant land ownership and registration details where legally accessible.
- 🔍 **Simplified Land Research:** Reduce the complexity of gathering property-related information.

### 🚀 Our Mission
Leverage technology to make land research simpler, smarter, and more accessible to everyone.
